# Set a time range

Statistics cover the whole capture by default. A [time range](#timeranges) limits a window to one part of the run.

To mark a range, hold Ctrl and drag over the timeline. The menu that opens applies the range to one of six windows: Find zone, Statistics, Flame graph, Wait stacks, Memory, Frame statistics. Right-clicking a zone, section or frame opens the same menu with that item's extent.

You can adjust a range by dragging its edges. The [Time range limits](#timerangelimits) window, under the * Tools* menu, lists all ranges.

Try it out: Ctrl-drag a part of your capture, pick Statistics from the menu, and compare the numbers with the full capture.
