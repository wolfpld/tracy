# Navigating the view

The capture may run for minutes, but the trouble you are looking for is over in microseconds. The mouse wheel can cross that gap, but only a little per scroll. The [navigation gestures](#navigatingview) are much faster.

1. To zoom into a region, drag the middle mouse button over it and release: the view jumps to the range you selected.
2. To zoom out, hold Ctrl and drag across a range: the current view collapses into the selected range.
3. To pan, drag the right mouse button. Panning works in both directions: sideways through time, vertically through the thread lanes.
4. To keep your hands on the keyboard, use A and D to scroll, W and S to zoom.

The time ruler above the timeline shows the exact time under the cursor.

When you zoom far out, zones thinner than a pixel are drawn with a zigzag fill. One zigzag bar can be many short zones, and you only find out by zooming in.

Now drag the middle mouse button over any stretch of your capture and watch it fill the screen. Try it out!
